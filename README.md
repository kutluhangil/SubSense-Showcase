## What it is

SubSense is a bilingual subscription tracker. It shows what you really spend each month, reminds you before every renewal and points out services that overlap. It is free, needs no account, and keeps your data on your device unless you choose to sync.

<img src="docs/media/divider.svg" width="100%" alt="">

## Highlights

| | |
|---|---|
| **Real monthly spend** | Monthly and yearly totals with budget alerts. |
| **Renewal reminders** | Local notifications before every charge. |
| **Calendar** | Every renewal on a month grid. |
| **Insights** | Category breakdowns and overlapping services. |
| **Explore** | Profiles and prices for popular services. |
| **Private by default** | Works offline without an account; Google sync is optional. |

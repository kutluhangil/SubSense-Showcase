## What it is

SubSense is a bilingual subscription tracker for Android. It shows what you really spend each month, reminds you before every renewal and points out services that overlap. It is free, has no ads, needs no account and keeps your data on the phone unless you choose to sync.

> **Why a showcase?** The Android app's code lives in a private repository. This one holds the exact Google Play assets so the app can be shown, linked and reviewed without opening the source. The same idea also runs on the web and on the Mac from the public [SubSense](https://github.com/kutluhangil/SubSense) monorepo.

## Highlights

| | |
|---|---|
| **Real monthly spend** | Monthly and yearly totals in your currency, with per-category budget alerts. |
| **Renewal reminders** | Notifications scheduled on the phone before every charge. No server. |
| **Price watch** | A plan whose price went up is flagged, measured on its US dollar price so exchange rates are not mistaken for hikes. |
| **On-device assistant** | Ask about spending, renewals, trials or overlaps in Turkish or English. Rule-based, no AI model, nothing leaves the phone. |
| **Calendar** | Every renewal on a month grid. |
| **Insights** | Category breakdowns, overlapping services and CSV export. |
| **Explore** | 42 service profiles with Turkish and global prices. |
| **App lock and widget** | Optional fingerprint unlock and a home-screen widget with the month's total. |
| **Private by default** | Works offline without an account; Google sync is optional and limited to your own data. |

## Türkçe özet

**SubSense**, aboneliklerini kolayca takip etmeni sağlayan, Türkçe ve İngilizce destekli, ücretsiz, reklamsız ve hesap gerektirmeyen bir Android abonelik uygulaması.
